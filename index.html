About me

Hi, I'm Shihaf. I'm in the first year of a B.Tech in Information Technology, which means most of what I know is only a few months old, and most of what I don't know is still waiting for me. I find that exciting more often than scary.

I'd rather be honest about the start than fake the finish.

I learn by building something small, breaking it, and then finding out why. That loop has already taught me more than any single lecture. This site is part of it: I wanted my first portfolio to feel like the first computer I wish I'd had, so I made the whole thing a desktop.

If you have a project that needs someone who asks questions and finishes what they start, I'd like to hear about it.
